---
title: YAP0037
name: Unsupported time zone
severity: warning
summary: format.dateTime() time zone is unknown to the runtime
---


`format.dateTime(..., { timeZone: '...' })` is called with a zone your `Intl` runtime doesn't know. yapyak renders the date in the system time zone.

Use a standard IANA time-zone identifier (e.g., `Europe/Stockholm`).
