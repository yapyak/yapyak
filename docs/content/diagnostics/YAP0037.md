---
title: YAP0037
name: Unsupported time zone
severity: warning
summary: format.dateTime() got a time zone the runtime doesn't know
---


`format.dateTime(..., { timeZone: '...' })` was called with a zone your `Intl` runtime doesn't know. yapyak rendered the date in the system time zone.

Use a standard IANA time-zone identifier (e.g., `Europe/Stockholm`).
