---
title: YAP0030
name: Forced locale invalid
severity: warning
summary: t.in() tag isn't a valid BCP 47 locale
---


`t.in('xx', 'source')` is called with a tag that isn't a valid BCP 47 locale. yapyak falls back to your `defaultLocale`.

Validate locale tags before passing them to `t.in()`, or check for typos in the configured locale list.
