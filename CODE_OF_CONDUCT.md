# Code of Conduct

> This project adopts the Contributor Covenant. The full text is at [contributor-covenant.org](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a [private vulnerability report](https://github.com/<owner>/<repo>/security/advisories/new) on GitHub. The form is intended for security vulnerabilities but doubles as the only confidential reporting channel this repo provides today.

Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards above. They may take corrective action — warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban — proportional to the violation.

The full enforcement guidelines are in the Contributor Covenant v2.1 § Enforcement Guidelines.
